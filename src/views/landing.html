Labor Ledger & Workplace Safety System

Every hour should count.

Fair pay. Safer workplaces. A voice for every worker.

In high-risk sectors like civil construction and sprawling campus facilities, informal workers routinely face unrecorded overtime, opaque pay deductions, and unaddressed safety hazards. Hisaab Barabar delivers deterministic, unalterable shift accounting, transparent payslip breakdowns, and direct hazard triage so that every worker receives the respect, compensation, and safety guaranteed by international human rights.

Configured Field Environments

Two Operating Sectors

Engineered with sector-specific labor rules, safety thresholds, and statutory deduction policies.

Heavy Infrastructure

Construction Sector

Metro Phase-3 Site (Pier 14–22). Heavy civil formwork, steel reinforcement, scaffolding, and concrete pouring.

  • Base Rate: ₹120.00 / hr
  • Standard Shift: 8.0 Hours (OT at 1.5× rate)
  • Fatigue Safety Cap: Flags alert at > 10.0 Hours
  • Welfare Cess: 4% Building Workers Fund
  • Primary Hazard: Falls from height & scaffold instability
Institutional Services

College & Campus

St. Jude Academic Campus. Facility sanitization, chemistry complex maintenance, perimeter security, and electrical panels.

  • Base Rate: ₹150.00 / hr
  • Standard Shift: 7.0 Hours (OT at 1.5× rate)
  • Fatigue Safety Cap: Flags alert at > 9.0 Hours
  • Welfare Cess: 5% Staff Welfare / ESI
  • Primary Hazard: Chemical vapor exposure & electrical wet zones
